# Compliance and safety

- Fetch only public resources intended for public access.
- Never bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or provider controls.
- Never scrape authenticated LinkedIn pages or store LinkedIn credentials.
- Preserve factual evidence and label inference as inference.
- Never claim that a contact is verified without verifiable source evidence.
- Cold outreach and important applications require human approval by default.
- Suppression, unsubscribe, bounce handling, and conservative send limits are required before outbound email is enabled.
